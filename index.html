Our Purpose

Care that extends
far beyond the bedside

At Emberlight Services, we believe care extends far beyond the bedside.

Every life holds an extraordinary collection of stories, wisdom, and moments worth preserving. While we proudly provide compassionate end-of-life support, our purpose reaches beyond personal care alone.

We believe families shouldn’t have to choose between caring for their loved one and managing the responsibilities of everyday life. By caring for both the individual and the home, we create space for families to step away from the to-do lists and step back into the moments that matter most.

Whether we’re offering practical support around the home, meaningful companionship, preserving a lifetime of memories through the Emberlight Legacy Collection Journal, or simply sitting quietly beside someone who needs a compassionate presence, every act of care is rooted in dignity, warmth, and connection.

Because in the end, our purpose isn’t simply to provide care.
It’s to give families the freedom to be together.

What We Do

We walk beside you —
long before the final days

Emberlight isn’t simply called during life’s final hours. We come alongside individuals and families months — or even years — before, building trusted relationships, easing the responsibilities of caregiving, preserving precious stories, and helping families navigate each season with confidence and compassion.

While hospice focuses on medical care, Emberlight focuses on the human experience — providing practical support, meaningful companionship, legacy preservation, caregiver respite, and guidance that allows families to spend less time managing responsibilities and more time simply loving one another.

Emberlight may be right for your family if you are:

  • Caring for a loved one at home and feeling the weight of it alone
  • Supporting someone with a serious illness who isn’t ready for hospice yet
  • Wanting to begin legacy work — capturing stories before it’s too late
  • A caregiver who simply needs time to rest, breathe, and be a family member again
  • Seeking a compassionate presence at the bedside when family cannot always be there
  • Wanting guidance navigating end-of-life before it arrives

Here’s what a typical Emberlight relationship looks like:

  • We start with a conversation — a free consult to learn your family’s needs, wishes, and concerns
  • We show up consistently — the same face, the same warmth, every visit
  • We handle the practical — light home care, errands, and daily tasks
  • We preserve what matters — through the Legacy Journal each visit
  • We collaborate with hospice — filling the human spaces beside medical care
  • We adapt as things change — your care plan evolves with your loved one

The principles that guide every visit:

  • Every person deserves to be seen, heard, and honored — regardless of faith, culture, or belief
  • Families should never have to choose between being present and keeping life going
  • Care is not always doing more — sometimes it is simply being there
  • Every story is worth preserving. Every legacy is worth remembering.
  • The final chapter deserves the same warmth and dignity as every other
  • No family should carry this alone

Meet the Founder

Hello, I’m Ona —
I’m glad you’re here.

I’m a mother of two beautiful daughters and have shared my life with my partner, Tavis, for nearly a decade. I was born and raised in the mountains of Oregon, where I developed a deep appreciation for nature, hard work, and the quiet moments that remind us what truly matters.

In 2009 I began working in senior care, and shortly after shifted into memory care — where I would spend years growing through nearly every role on the floor. I started as a dishwasher and caregiver in assisted living, and over time became a Medication Technician, Activities Director, Resident Care Coordinator, and Medication Room Manager, while also stepping in to support our Executive Director with a wide range of responsibilities. Those years taught me that true care isn’t measured only by medical tasks, but by presence, compassion, and treating every person with dignity.

Over the years, I have held countless hands during life’s final moments, administered medications, provided one-on-one end-of-life care, supported grieving families, and sat quietly beside people as they took their last breath. Those experiences have shaped not only the caregiver I am today, but the person I strive to be.

Outside of my work, you’ll often find me tending my garden, baking, creating herbal remedies from plants I’ve grown and harvested, or exploring the Oregon mountains that have always felt like home. I believe healing often begins with slowing down, listening deeply, and finding beauty in life’s simplest moments.

I welcome and honor people of every faith, culture, belief system, and spiritual tradition. Every person’s journey is unique, and it is a privilege to support each family with compassion, respect, and an open heart.

Emberlight Services was created from the belief that every life deserves to be honored, every story deserves to be remembered, and every family deserves the opportunity to be fully present with the people they love.

I am here to step in, so you can step closer.

What Makes Us Different

The Emberlight
Legacy Collection Journal

Most end-of-life care focuses on comfort in the present. We also help preserve a lifetime.

The Emberlight Legacy Collection Journal is the heart of Emberlight Services and the signature of our care. Included with each qualifying care plan, it is far more than a journal — it is a guided journey through a person’s life: their memories, relationships, values, accomplishments, and the hopes that still remain.

Each week, we dedicate intentional one-on-one time to meaningful conversation, documenting the stories, wisdom, and experiences that have shaped your loved one’s life. Together, we create a keepsake your family will treasure for generations.

But the journal doesn’t stop with reflection. As we learn what matters most, we begin discovering the wishes that still live in the heart — the conversations waiting to happen, the places worth revisiting, the traditions worth passing on. When possible, we help bring those wishes to life.

Together we may…

  • ✦ Preserve cherished family stories and traditions
  • ✦ Record favorite recipes and the memories behind them
  • ✦ Write letters for children, grandchildren, or loved ones
  • ✦ Reconnect with someone important
  • ✦ Visit meaningful places
  • ✦ Reflect on life’s lessons, accomplishments, and defining moments
  • ✦ Explore hopes, wishes, and experiences still waiting to be fulfilled
  • ✦ Create space for gratitude, forgiveness, peace, and closure
